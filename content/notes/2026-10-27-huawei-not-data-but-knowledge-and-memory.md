## The Core Concept: Why Data is Not Enough

Huawei’s strategy argues that in the AI and agent-driven era, raw storage capacity is no longer the main objective. Instead, infrastructure must actively refine information: [1](https://aimagazine.com/news/how-is-huawei-addressing-the-long-context-memory-problem)

- **From Capacity to Value:** Standard storage just holds "scattered data". AI demands that infrastructure continuously transform this data into high-quality datasets, effectively turning information into callable knowledge and memory. [1](https://aimagazine.com/news/how-is-huawei-addressing-the-long-context-memory-problem)
- **Causal Accuracy:** The technological direction relies on novel memory systems featuring "causal accuracy and trustworthy traceability". This allows AI agents to retrieve context reliably during training and inference. [1](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2026/9/hc-intelligent-world-2035), [2](https://www.aol.com/articles/huaweis-latest-report-proposes-10-134100000.html), [3](https://aimagazine.com/news/how-is-huawei-addressing-the-long-context-memory-problem)
- **The AI Data Infrastructure:** Huawei is rolling out hardware architectures (like specialized data lakes and knowledge platforms) intended to change storage from a passive "capacity center" into an active "value release center". [1](https://www.huawei.com/en/publications/huaweitech/202601), [2](https://aimagazine.com/news/how-is-huawei-addressing-the-long-context-memory-problem)

## Overview of Huawei's AI Storage Shift

| Strategic Focus | Past Paradigm (Data Era) | Future Paradigm (AI / AGI Era) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Resource | Raw, scattered data | High-quality Knowledge and Memory |
| Storage Role | Passive capacity center | Active value release center |
| System Feature | Simple file/block retrieval | Causal accuracy & trustworthy traceability |
| End Goal | Information retention | Powering autonomous AGI & Agent OS |
